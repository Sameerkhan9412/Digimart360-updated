Product Summary

Acme Fluid Systems SS304 Series Ball Valves are engineered for critical industrial isolation applications across chemical plants, oil & gas lines, processing facilities, and high-pressure liquid transfer systems. Precision-machined investment cast body with blowout-proof stem ensures maximum operator safety under pressurized cycles.

  • 100% hydrostatically and pneumatically pressure tested to API 598 standards.
  • Reinforced PTFE seats for zero seat leakage and low operating torque.
  • Standard ISO 5211 top mounting pad ready for electrical/pneumatic actuator retrofitting.
