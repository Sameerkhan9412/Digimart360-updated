Questions buyers ask about ball valves

Answers from our category team, based on current listings.

Which Indian cities have the most ball valve manufacturers?

Ahmedabad (Odhav and Vatva GIDC), Rajkot, Mumbai, Pune (Bhosari MIDC) and Vadodara together account for about 70% of the ball valve suppliers on DIGI MART 360. Rajkot leads in brass valves; Ahmedabad and Vadodara in stainless steel and actuated valves.

Why do ball valve prices vary so much?

Material, size and pressure class drive most of the difference. A ½" forged brass valve starts around ₹180, while a 12" Class 600 trunnion valve can cross ₹2 lakh. Testing (API 598, IBR), third-party inspection and certificates also add cost.

What should I check before ordering?

Ask for the material test certificate (MTC), the hydro-test report, the body and seat material, and the end connection standard (ANSI B16.5 flanges or BSP/NPT threads). On DIGI MART 360, filter for GST-verified and DM Trusted suppliers.

Can I get a sample before a bulk order?

Most manufacturers send a paid sample for standard sizes. Mention it in your requirement and suppliers will include sample pricing in their quote.
